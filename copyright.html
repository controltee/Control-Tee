1. What this page covers

This notice explains who owns what, how that work may be used, and how a client or rights holder can request that a piece be removed.

Unless a project page states otherwise, final creative deliverables shown on this site were produced by Control Tee under a commercial engagement with the named client, and ownership or licensing of those deliverables is governed by the agreement between Control Tee and that client, not by this website. Displaying a project here is done for portfolio and promotional purposes and, unless a client has specifically requested otherwise, with the understanding that Control Tee retains the right to showcase work it produced.

Site elements that are not client deliverables, including the website's own design, layout, code, animations, and the Control Tee brand identity itself, are the property of Control Tee.

3. Permitted use

Content on this site may be viewed for personal, non-commercial browsing. It may not be downloaded, reproduced, redistributed, or used for commercial purposes, including by competitors or third-party marketers, without prior written permission from Control Tee and, where applicable, the relevant client.

4. Takedown requests

If you are a client (or an authorised representative of a client) featured on this site and would like a project removed, updated, or credited differently, contact us with:

  • The name of the client/brand and the project in question (or a link to the page)
  • What you'd like changed: removal, correction, or a credit/attribution update
  • Confirmation that you are authorised to make this request on behalf of the brand

We aim to act on legitimate takedown requests from clients or their authorised representatives promptly, generally within a reasonable timeframe of receiving a verifiable request.

5. Reporting infringement

If you believe content on this site infringes your intellectual property rights in some other way, contact us with a description of the work, its location on the site, and the basis for your claim, and we will review and respond.

6. Contact us

Takedown requests and copyright questions can be sent through our Contact page.
