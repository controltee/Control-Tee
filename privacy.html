Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

1. Who we are

Control Tee ("Control Tee", "we", "us", "our") is a creative design studio based in Nairobi, Kenya, offering motion design, brand identity, poster design, social and marketing campaigns, and video editing services. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Contact form submissions. When you use the contact form on our Contact page, we collect the name, email address, project type, and message you provide.
  • Bot/spam verification. The contact form uses an hCaptcha challenge and a hidden honeypot field to filter out automated spam submissions before they reach us.
  • Standard technical logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard request information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability purposes, in the same way most web hosts do.
  • Admin/CMS accounts. Staff who log into our content management system (admin.html) authenticate with an email and password through Supabase Authentication. This applies only to Control Tee staff, not to visitors of the public site.

We do not run first-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not use cookies of our own.

3. How your information is used

Contact form details are used solely to respond to your enquiry and to discuss a potential project with you. We do not use contact form data for marketing you did not request, and we do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Where your information is stored

Contact form submissions are delivered to us through Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service that emails the submission to us; Web3Forms is a data processor for this purpose and handles delivery according to its own privacy practices. Our portfolio content (project images, videos, and site text) is stored and served through Supabase (a Postgres database and object storage provider). Contact form submissions themselves are not additionally stored in our Supabase database.

5. Cookies and third-party services

This site does not set first-party tracking or analytics cookies. The following third-party service may set its own cookies when you use the contact form:

  • hCaptcha — used on the Contact page to distinguish human visitors from automated bots. hCaptcha may set cookies or use similar technology as part of that verification. This is a functional/security measure, not behavioural advertising tracking.

Because this is the only third-party cookie-setting component on the site, and it exists purely to prevent spam rather than to track you, we have not published a separate cookie policy; this section serves that purpose. If that changes (for example, if analytics are added in future), this policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Data retention

We retain contact form enquiries for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and to keep a record of prospective client communications, after which they may be deleted. Portfolio content stored in Supabase is retained for as long as it remains part of our published or archived body of work.

7. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have rights in relation to your personal data, including the right to be informed of its use, to access it, to request correction or deletion, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC). To exercise any of these rights in relation to information you have sent us, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

This site is intended for businesses and individuals seeking creative services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices or applicable law change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or how your information is handled can be sent through our Contact page.
